# ForgeTrace – Pitch Deck (10 slides, ~5 minutes)

**Slide 1 – Title**
ForgeTrace — *From RFQ to a scannable quality passport.* Team names · college · logo.

**Slide 2 – Problem**
Sourcing custom parts in India still runs on phone calls, WhatsApp and Excel.
Weeks to find a supplier · quotes impossible to compare · no visibility during production · quality proof is paper.
(Add 1 cited statistic about MSMEs / manufacturing.)

**Slide 3 – Solution**
One platform: verified manufacturers, matched RFQs, side-by-side quotes, live production board,
inspected batches with a public QR passport. *Screenshot: quotation comparison.*

**Slide 4 – How it works (demo flow)**
Post RFQ → matched suppliers quote → accept → machine booked → batch inspected → QR passport → pay → ship.
*Screenshots: production board + passport page on a phone.*

**Slide 5 – Why now / why us**
Digital procurement adoption by SMEs · traceability & compliance demands · Make-in-India supplier growth.
Our edge: real machine-capacity data + accumulated inspection history.

**Slide 6 – Market**
TAM / SAM / SOM from the Lean Canvas (with sources). Beachhead: one industrial cluster + hardware startups.

**Slide 7 – Business model**
3–5 % commission on completed orders · manufacturer Pro subscription · traceability add-on.
Example: [N] orders × ₹[avg order] × 4 % = ₹[monthly revenue] (state assumptions).

Stack: Spring Boot 4 · PostgreSQL · Docker · AWS (EC2, RDS, S3, CloudWatch).
