About

Making manufacturing sourcing simple and traceable

The problem

Small and medium manufacturers often have machine capacity that sits idle, while buyers spend days calling suppliers, comparing quotes over email and chasing production updates. Once parts are delivered, there is rarely a clear record of how and where each batch was made and checked.

What ForgeTrace does

  • Connects industrial buyers with verified job-work manufacturers through RFQs and quotations.
  • Matches RFQs to manufacturers by process and material, so suppliers only see relevant work.
  • Schedules real machine capacity and prevents double bookings.
  • Records production status, batches and quality inspections in one place.
  • Gives every approved batch a QR passport that buyers can scan and share.

Who it is for

Industrial buyers, startups building hardware, and small and medium job-work manufacturers offering processes such as CNC machining, laser cutting, fabrication and 3D printing.

About this project

ForgeTrace is being built as a university startup project by a team of B.Tech Information Technology students. The current version is a working prototype. Payments run in test mode only and no real transactions take place.
