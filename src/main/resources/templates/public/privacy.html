Privacy notice

Prototype document prepared for a university project. It must be reviewed by a qualified legal advisor (for example against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) before commercial use.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: name, work email, phone number (optional) and a securely hashed password.
  • Company data: company name, address, registration number and GSTIN (optional).
  • Business records: RFQs, drawings you upload, quotations, orders, batches, inspections, payments (test mode) and shipments.
  • Technical data: login times and IP addresses recorded in our audit log for security.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account and the sourcing, production and traceability features you use.
  • To verify manufacturers before they appear to buyers.
  • To keep the platform secure and investigate misuse.
  • To reply to messages sent through the contact form.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

3. Who can see it

  • Buyers see the public profile of verified manufacturers.
  • Manufacturers see the RFQs that match their capabilities, including attached drawings.
  • A product passport shows only approved public batch information - never personal details or private drawings.
  • Platform administrators can access records to verify, support and secure the service.

4. How we protect it

  • Passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes, never in plain text.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest on the cloud database and storage.
  • Access is restricted by role and by organization on the server.

5. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is active. Financial, audit and traceability records are kept for the period required for legal and quality purposes, even after an account is closed.

6. Your choices

You can view and update your details on your profile page. To request a copy of your data or to close your account, contact us through the contact page.
