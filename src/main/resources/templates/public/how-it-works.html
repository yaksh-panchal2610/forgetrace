How it works

One workflow for buyers, manufacturers and quality teams

Each step creates a record that stays linked to the order and the batch.

For buyers

1

Register

Create a buyer account for your company in a few minutes.

2

Post an RFQ

Choose a process and material, set quantity, delivery date and quote deadline.

3

Accept the best quote

Compare offers by total price and lead time. Accepting creates a production order.

4

Track and receive

Follow production, inspection and shipment. Scan the QR passport on delivery.

For manufacturers

1

Get verified

Register your facility and capabilities. An admin reviews and verifies your company.

2

Add machines

List machines one by one or import a CSV file. Set working hours and maintenance.

3

Quote matching RFQs

Only RFQs that match your processes and materials reach your inbox.

4

Produce and ship

Reserve machine time, create batches, send them for inspection and dispatch.

Quality and traceability

Measured inspections

Inspectors record each measurement against its minimum and maximum. Any failed measurement blocks approval.

QR product passport

Approved batches get a passport with a random, unguessable link. It shows only approved public information.

Recalls

If a problem is found later, admins can recall a batch and every affected organization is notified.

A ForgeTrace passport is a platform record, not an independent or government certification of product quality.
