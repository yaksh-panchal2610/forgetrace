Terms of use

Prototype document prepared for a university project. It must be reviewed by a qualified legal advisor before commercial use.

1. The service

ForgeTrace is a platform that connects buyers with manufacturers and records production, inspection and delivery information. ForgeTrace is not a party to the contract between a buyer and a manufacturer.

2. Accounts

  • You must give accurate information and keep your password secret.
  • Manufacturers must be verified before they can receive RFQs. Verification is a platform check, not a guarantee of quality.
  • We may suspend accounts that provide false information or misuse the platform.

3. RFQs, quotations and orders

  • A quotation is valid until its expiry date. Expired quotations cannot be accepted.
  • Accepting a quotation creates a production order between the buyer and the manufacturer.
  • Prices are shown in full, including setup fee and tax, before you accept. There are no hidden charges.

4. Payments

In this prototype, payments run in test (sandbox) mode only. No real money is charged.

5. Drawings and intellectual property

Buyers keep ownership of the drawings and specifications they upload. Manufacturers may use them only to prepare a quotation and to fulfil an accepted order.

6. Reviews

Only buyers with a real order can review the manufacturer for that order, once per order. Fake, paid or misleading reviews are not allowed and will be removed.

7. Product passports

A ForgeTrace product passport is a record of information entered on the platform. It is not an independent, third-party or government certification.

8. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through the contact page.
